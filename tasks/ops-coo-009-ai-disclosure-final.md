## What I Personally Decided

- The core thesis: Single Grain does not need more AI output; it needs a score-to-route operating loop that turns output into accepted work, owner action, memory, and compounding improvement.
- The first 48-hour priority order: ClickFlow churn, stale prospects, queue routing, then Oracle keyword source checks.
- The risk model: auto-execute only reversible, source-verified, known-pattern work; route ambiguous high-value work to owners or expert councils; keep Eric on red decisions.
- The cron audit posture: kill or pause low-action/high-noise workflows instead of keeping every automation alive.
- The evidence posture: use HeavisideOS Slack assistant evals as the primary measured AI workflow proof, while treating x-accel, SloaneVault, and decision councils as supporting operating primitives.
- The maturity boundary: x-accel is separate from HeavisideOS; HeavisideOS is early practical Slack/client/performance summarization plus company-brain direction, not a fully mature company operating system.
- The redaction rule: raw internal reports or private source records should not be attached externally without removing customer/client identifiers.
