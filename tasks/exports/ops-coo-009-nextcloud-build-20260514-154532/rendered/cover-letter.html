# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Single Grain team,

I was not looking for a new role. This one found me.

I follow Eric's content. He is already one of the reference points in my AI agency and company-brain council of experts. When I saw the challenge, I thought it would be fun to test myself against it. Then I read the role more closely. It maps almost exactly to the work I have been doing in my own companies.

I founded Heaviside in 2011 and have run it across SEO, web, digital marketing, and related services. Over the last two years I have rebuilt much of the company around software, AI, and operator-owned workflows. Revenue per FTE has nearly doubled since 2023 while margin improved. My existing businesses now take less than five hours a week to manage because the team and systems run the day-to-day.

The problem in the COO challenge is the problem I keep working on: AI output is cheap. Turning it into accepted work, owner action, revenue, retention, and memory is the hard part. My response lays out how I would do that at Single Grain: protect Eric's time, close loops, kill low-signal automation, and turn ClickFlow, Karrot, and agent workflows into real TES revenue.
