## Part 2 - Risk Tiering

Every output gets two passes `[Assumed]`: action value and risk. Action value scores revenue proximity, time sensitivity, source confidence, owner clarity, effort size, and learning value on a 0/1/2 scale `[Assumed]`. Risk flags are irreversible customer commitment, strategic relationship, public/brand risk, legal/privacy/security, financial exposure, novelty, and low source confidence.

Green: value >= 7 `[Assumed]`, source verified, reversible, known pattern, no red flags. Examples: verified existing-page SEO fixes, approved-template Flash repurposing, approved non-strategic Cyborg follow-up, and Alfred internal briefings.

Yellow: value >= 5 `[Assumed]` or uncertainty without CEO-specific risk. Owner review handles domain judgment; expert councils handle high-value ambiguity like creative refreshes, channel pivots, SEO positioning, or paid media calls.

Red: Eric only. Pricing, refunds, contracts, strategic accounts, public claims, legal/privacy/security, senior people decisions, new core crons, killing core crons, or CEO-level priority tradeoffs. This moves Eric from 15+ daily review items `[Observed from brief]` toward 3-5 real decisions `[Observed from brief]`.

When green fails: reverse if possible, notify the owner, move that item type to yellow, add a memory record, and change the rule.

## Operational Experience

I have been running Heaviside since 2011 `[Observed applicant claim]`. We sell and deliver SEO, web design, digital marketing, and related services. Today it is roughly a $750K/year business `[Estimated applicant claim]`. The recurring bottleneck has not been ideas; it has been the manual work around selling, reporting, analysis, and fulfillment.

That is why I have built a lot of internal tools. Two examples are HG Market Report and HG SEO Commander. HG Market Report replaced manual data pulls and deck building with a live generated customer report, sales presentation, and follow-up sequence. It saves roughly 1-2 hours per sales report `[Estimated applicant claim]` and gives buyers a path when they want the analysis but do not want to talk to sales yet. HG SEO Commander does the same thing on the delivery side: SEO analysis, reporting, GBP posts, blog posts, and link-building deliverables. It saves at least 3-4 hours per client per month `[Estimated applicant claim]`.

The adoption lesson was simple: people use systems that remove work. The operators did not fight these tools because the tools took away the worst parts of the job. When a bug created bad GBP posts, someone started deleting them by hand. I fixed it through the CLI and showed them how to handle it that way going forward. They were excited because the new process made them more capable instead of adding another layer of bureaucracy.
