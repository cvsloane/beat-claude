## Part A - Operational Experience Interview

Goal: replace the too-narrow migration example with the strongest true operator story. The likely anchor is 10+ years running an agency, supported by PVM/GDM/Heaviside examples.

Answer in bullets. Rough is fine.

### A1. Agency Operating Scope

For the agency you have run for 10+ years:

- What agency/entities should we name or avoid naming?
- Approximate years active.
- Services sold/delivered.
- Your role: owner, GM, operator, strategist, sales, delivery, finance, hiring, etc.
- Team size / contractor bench / vendors, if safe to say.
- Client count, niche, revenue range, ad spend managed, or other scale markers, if safe to say.
- What operating problem kept recurring?

### A2. Broken Rhythm You Fixed

Pick the best example of a broken or nonexistent operating rhythm you installed.

- What was broken before?
- What cadence/mechanism did you install?
- Who had to follow it?
- How did you enforce it?
- What changed afterward?

### A3. Process Enforcement / Resistance

Pick one process people resisted, ignored, or kept bypassing.

- The process.
- Who resisted or ignored it?
- Why did they resist?
- What made compliance easier than bypassing the process?
- What was the result?

### A4. Cross-Workstream Coordination

Pick one example where you kept multiple departments/workstreams moving at once.

- Workstreams involved.
- Mechanism used to keep state.
- How priorities were resolved.
- What proof shows it worked.

## Part B - MMPI-Style Self-Assessment Interview

Goal: write a direct, non-clinical self-awareness section. This is not a medical or psychological test. The brief asks for an LLM-assisted "MMPI-style" reflection, meaning strengths, risks, and compensating mechanisms.

Answer in bullets.

### B1. Operating Style

- In work, are you more builder, fixer, strategist, closer, manager, analyst, or operator?
- What do you do better than most people?
- What types of work drain you or make you impatient?
- What do people repeatedly come to you for?

### B2. Failure Modes

- When stressed, do you over-control, detach, move too fast, delay, avoid conflict, create too many systems, or something else?
- What do you tend to under-delegate?
- What kinds of teammates frustrate you?
- What feedback have you received more than once?

### B3. Founder / CEO Fit

- What kind of CEO do you work best with?
- How do you handle high-velocity founders who change direction quickly?
- What decisions should stay with the CEO?
- What decisions should be delegated to you?

### B4. Compensation Mechanisms

- What personal operating rules keep you effective?
- What systems do you use to prevent overbuilding, dropped follow-through, or unclear ownership?
- What should Single Grain know about how to manage you well?
