## Strongest Counterargument

The packet can still lose clarity if every internal tool artifact gets uploaded. Names like HeavisideOS, Hermes, x-accel, SloaneVault, HG SEO Commander, and decision councils are valuable internally, but a blind reviewer needs a simple path:

1. Can this person run the agency operating system?
2. Has he moved the revenue/FTE and margin metric before?
3. Has he built real automation systems?
4. Does he understand Eric's specific operating gap?

Anything that does not help that first read should be appendix-only or held for follow-up.

## What The Council Missed

The first council correctly flagged proof bloat but did not define a hard upload hierarchy. The second council's main addition is the packaging rule:

- Required: written answer, AI disclosure, candidate profile.
- Core proof: operating artifact, evidence log, attachment map, RPE/profitability summary, sales/delivery automation summary.
- Appendix only: HeavisideOS Slack eval and Hermes scheduler-fleet summary.
- Backup: x-accel, decision councils, SloaneVault memory, raw screenshots, raw exports.
