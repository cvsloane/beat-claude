## Operating Artifact

Command queue states:

- `dead`: not worth action or too stale.
- `source-check`: possibly useful, but source data must be verified first.
- `green`: auto-executes because source is verified, action is reversible, pattern is known, and owner plus writeback path are clear.
- `yellow`: owner or GM judgment required, but no CEO context needed.
- `red`: CEO decision required because of pricing, trust, legal, privacy, security, senior people, strategic accounts, public claims, or core cron changes.

Daily cadence:

- Morning source refresh: Mission Control, CRM, ClickFlow, GSC/GA4, agent queue, expired outreach.
- Morning assignment: every yellow item gets owner, next action, and due time.
- Same-day review: churn, stale pipeline, and customer-sensitive issues clear before content and lower-risk backlog.
- CEO block: only red items, target 3-5 per day.
- End-of-day closeout: executed, rejected, owner-assigned, or memory/rule update. No orphan items.

Cron audit rule:

- Keep what produces accepted work.
- Improve what has business value but too much noise.
- Kill what creates review debt without action.

Initial sample calls:

- Kill or pause: Competitor Pricing Monitor, Candidate Outreach Follow-up.
- Improve: GSC Quick Win Scan, LinkedIn Prospect Sourcer, Churn Signal Detector.
- Keep and tighten: Deal Revival Scan, Weekly Content Calendar, Content Repurpose.
