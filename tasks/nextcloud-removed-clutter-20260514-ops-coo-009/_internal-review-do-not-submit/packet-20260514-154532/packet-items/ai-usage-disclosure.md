## What I Personally Decided

- The core thesis: AI output only matters when it becomes accepted work, owner action, memory, margin, retention, and revenue/FTE leverage.
- The first 48-hour priority order: ClickFlow churn first, stale prospects second, queue routing third, Oracle keyword source checks fourth.
- The green/yellow/red routing model: green only for reversible, source-verified, known-pattern work; yellow for owner/council judgment; red for Eric-level trust, pricing, strategy, public, legal, people, and key relationship decisions.
- The cron audit posture: kill or pause low-action/high-noise workflows instead of keeping every automation alive.
- The evidence posture: lead with Heaviside RPE/profitability proof, support with HG Market Report, HG SEO Commander, SloaneVault, x-accel, decision councils, and carefully bounded HeavisideOS proof.
- The cron proof posture: use Hermes/open-agents as evidence of operating a monitored scheduler fleet, not as proof that every scheduled job deserves to exist.
- The maturity boundary: x-accel is separate from HeavisideOS; HeavisideOS is early practical Slack/client/performance summarization plus company-brain direction, not a fully adopted company operating system.
- The redaction rule: raw internal reports, P&L exports, CRM data, Slack messages, and private vault notes should not be attached externally without redaction.
