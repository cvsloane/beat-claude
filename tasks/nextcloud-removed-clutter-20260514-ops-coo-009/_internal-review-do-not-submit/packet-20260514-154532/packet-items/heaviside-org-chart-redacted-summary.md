## Leadership Read

The leadership proof is not that the team exists on a website. The stronger point is that the team is organized into functional ownership areas that can run recurring delivery without day-to-day founder intervention:

- Chris has sustained and grown the agency while holding a full-time PASCO role, which only works if the team can own recurring delivery without founder bottlenecking.
- Chris's existing businesses take less than `5` hours/week to manage, which makes the founder-leverage claim concrete.
- This should be framed as delegated operating-capacity proof, not as a claim that Chris intends to split attention casually. The point is that existing businesses are built to keep running through owners and systems while Chris takes on a new operating role.
- Technical operations and web have named owners.
- SEO, PPC, and local visibility have named functional specialists.
- Operations has a management layer responsible for project flow, task monitoring, process, reporting, and admin support.
- Client relationship / client success has customer-facing coverage.
- Internal ownership docs show work mapped to operators rather than every recurring decision routing back to Chris.

## Use In GM Packet

This artifact supports one narrow, useful claim:

> Heaviside has a real operating bench across technical operations, web, SEO, PPC, operations, project flow, client coordination, and reporting. The founder-leverage claim is therefore not just "I use AI"; it is "I hired and organized a team that can own recurring delivery with less than `5` hours/week of founder management, sustained that while also holding a full-time PASCO role, then used software and automation to increase leverage inside that team."

Do not overclaim this as independent proof of exact founder hours. For that, use calendar/time evidence or operator references if needed.
