# Anonymized Council Notes: Path To 10/10

## Decision

What would it actually take to get the Single Grain GM / `ops-coo-009` application packet from roughly 9.5 to a true 10/10 across likely scoring, Eric-founder skim, presentation, proof, and application fit? Distinguish real score lift from perfectionist churn.

## A

### Thesis

The only real path from 9.5 to true 10 is not rewriting the answer. It is increasing trust density: one optional, redacted proof walkthrough that makes the private claims inspectable without making the official packet heavier.

### Reasoning

The written answer already beats Claude on judgment, first-48 sequencing, cron kills, AI boundaries, and founder-load reduction. The remaining cap is evidentiary, not verbal: private P&L/FTE claims, workflow claims, global source labels, and delegated-capacity claims all ask the reviewer to believe Chris until interview. A tight proof layer could lift scores: 3-5 minute walkthrough or one live-page section showing redacted P&L method, workflow screens, job/log manifests, and accepted-work examples. That addresses proof, Eric skim, and application fit because it says: this is not a clever packet; this is how he actually operates.

### Biggest risk

The proof artifact becomes another internal-systems tour and creates more work for the reviewer. That would lower presentation score and trigger rubric gaming.

### Where I may be wrong

A strict reviewer may not click anything beyond the PDF. If so, the gain is capped until live walkthrough or reference checks. A true 10 may be impossible pre-interview without third-party/operator verification.

### Recommended action

Choose C in the smallest possible form: leave the official 2-page answer alone, add one optional proof walkthrough/map, and stop. Do not restructure, add raw records, bracket-label every number, or expand the appendix.
