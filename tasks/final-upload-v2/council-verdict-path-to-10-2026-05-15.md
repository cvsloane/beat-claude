# Council Verdict: Path To 10/10

## Problem

The current Single Grain GM / `ops-coo-009` packet is already scoring around `9.5/10` on challenge-read and stronger than Claude on lived proof. The question is what would actually move it to a true `10/10` across scoring, Eric-founder skim, presentation, proof, and application fit without creating proof bloat or making the packet feel engineered for the rubric.

## Council Composition

Six-advisor council: Contrarian, First Principles Thinker, Expansionist, Outsider, Executor, Wisdom of the Crowd.

## Crowd Signal

- Live X/crowd signal was attempted through the local X path, but the searches hit the monthly budget limit.
- Existing stored council notes pointed in the same direction: the packet improves when proof is easier to verify, not when the written answer gets longer.

## Recommended Verdict

Do not rewrite the packet. A true `10/10` is not unlocked by more prose. It is unlocked by trust transfer: one small proof path that makes the private claims inspectable without making the official upload heavier.

The submitted packet should remain lean. The proof path should be optional, private or semi-private, and ready for interview/source-check.

## Why

- The current answer already covers the scoring surface: first `48` hours, risk gate, cron decisions, AI limits, failure modes, personality fit, revenue/FTE, founder leverage, and lived operating proof.
- The remaining cap is verification, not writing. Revenue/FTE, margin, workflow savings, Hermes/open-agents, and delegated operating capacity are strong claims, but they are still mostly self-attested until someone sees records, logs, artifacts, or hears from an operator/reference.
- Trying to force all proof into the upload would hurt the exact thing the brief rewards: complexity reduction. It would add internal names, source clutter, and reviewer fatigue.

## Strongest Counterargument

A literal rubric reviewer may penalize the answer for not inline-labeling every number or may not click a live page. If that happens, a small amount of source-label friction remains. But bracketed labels throughout the answer would likely hurt Eric-skim and presentation more than they help scoring.

## What The Council Missed

The first council round over-converged on “make a proof artifact” without defining the minimum viable version or the stop rule. The reviewer layer corrected this: the proof path only helps if it reduces uncertainty without adding cognitive load.

## Disagreements / Unresolved Questions

- Whether the proof path should be lightly visible in the upload, linked from the support file, or held entirely for interview.
- Whether one additional source-clarity sentence is worth adding if it replaces existing friction and does not increase length.
- Whether an operator/reference quote is available quickly enough to use without slowing the submission.

## Next Step

Prepare one fixed `5`-minute proof walkthrough with four artifacts: P&L/FTE method, one sales or delivery workflow, Hermes/open-agents job evidence, and delegated operating-capacity/talent proof.
