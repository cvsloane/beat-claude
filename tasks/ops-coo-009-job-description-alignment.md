- Use AI, automation, dashboards, standardized processes, and performance management systems.

Negative screen:

- Managing teams without improving systems.
- Coordinating projects rather than building infrastructure.
- Relying on founder involvement.
- Growing headcount without improving efficiency.

Positive screen:

- Operated/scaled a performance marketing agency or service business.
- Built systems that significantly improved productivity.
- Strong financial discipline.
- Built leadership teams that operate independently.
- Used AI/automation to increase operational leverage.
