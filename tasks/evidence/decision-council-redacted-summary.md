## Workflow

The council process includes:

1. Gate check: use council only for sufficiently ambiguous/high-stakes decisions.
2. Normalize the decision into a required input shape.
3. Convene six advisor lenses in parallel.
4. Include Wisdom of the Crowd when public signal is relevant and available.
5. Anonymize advisor responses before peer review.
6. Run reviewer pass without revealing advisor identity.
7. Produce chairman synthesis with one recommendation and one next step.

## Transfer To Single Grain

Use councils for yellow-tier decisions such as:

- paid media creative refresh or scaling calls,
- channel pivots,
- content/SEO positioning,
- ambiguous offer/pricing framing before it becomes a red Eric decision,
- cross-functional operational tradeoffs where one owner view is too narrow.

Do not use councils for:

- green routine outputs,
- obvious source-check failures,
- stale/no-value items,
- urgent red CEO calls.

## Caveats

- Decision councils improve judgment but are not source-of-truth systems.
- They can become delay theater if used on daily queue noise.
- They should end with a decision, owner, and next action, not an essay.
