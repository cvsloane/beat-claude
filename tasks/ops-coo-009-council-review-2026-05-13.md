Assess whether the current Single Grain GM / `ops-coo-009` packet is factually correct, properly understood, strategically strong, and likely to beat the published Claude baseline. The council also reviewed whether the late Hermes/open-agents scheduler-fleet proof should be added.

## Recommended Verdict

Do a targeted packaging/evidence pass, not a substantive overhaul. The packet is likely stronger than Claude because it has real operating proof, an inspectable artifact, source-labeled numbers, and a clear GM thesis.

## Why

- It answers the brief directly: churn first, stale prospects second, queue routing third, concrete green/yellow/red rules, two sample cron kills, failure modes, human boundaries, AI disclosure, and number labels.
- Claude is competent on triage and cron audit, but weak where this packet is strongest: lived agency operating proof, revenue/FTE discipline, artifact depth, self-assessment, and proof-backed AI operating examples.
- The Heaviside RPE/profitability proof is the primary differentiator. It should be framed as metric fluency and trajectory, not as equivalence to Single Grain's `300K-400K` target.
- The Hermes/open-agents fleet materially strengthens the cron section: `88` scheduled jobs in live state as of 2026-05-13 20:42 ET, with `64` enabled, `24` paused/disabled, `86` ok, and `2` error.

## Strongest Counterargument

The proof stack can feel overpacked. Names like HeavisideOS, x-accel, SloaneVault, HG SEO Commander, Hermes, and decision councils may read like internal jargon unless every attachment is plainly labeled by what it proves. Redacted internal proof can also create credibility anxiety if the reviewer cannot quickly understand what is observed, estimated, or assumed.

## What The Council Missed

The first advisor pass underweighted operational-experience proof. The Hermes/open-agents scheduler fleet is directly relevant to the challenge's `48` crons, but it must be caveated as bounded internal scheduler-fleet experience, not enterprise-scale proof and not proof that every cron is high-value.
